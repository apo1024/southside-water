# South Side Clean Water

Static site (GitHub Pages) for water & air purifier rentals on Chicago's South Side, by Dreamlore LLC.

- `index.html` — page layout and styles
- `app.js` — settings at the top (name, phone, email, taxes, ZIP codes, `authorized`), texts in English and Spanish
- `products.js` — products, plans and prices from the Cuckoo Rental America price sheet (confirm before quoting)
- `img/` — product photos

Quote requests open the visitor's own SMS / email app; nothing is stored on the site.
