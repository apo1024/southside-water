Questions

What is the difference between self-care and visit-care?

Self-care: you receive the new filters and change them yourself (easy, a few minutes). Visit-care: a technician comes to clean and change filters. Some models are visit-care only.

Do I need to own my home?

No. Countertop and standing models don't need any drilling. Ask me about your situation.

What happens at the end of the contract?

After a 5- or 6-year contract, the product becomes yours. With a 3-year contract you can keep renting at a lower price (WeCare program).

Are the prices final?

Prices come from the current Cuckoo Rental America price sheet and can change. Your final price is confirmed before you sign; approval and the contract are with Cuckoo Rental America.
